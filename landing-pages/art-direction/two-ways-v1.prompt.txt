Develop a distinct PPQ identity rather than copying any site. Think warm charcoal, copper, controlled studio illumination and beautifully detailed software. The design must feel much more expressive and finished than a plain gray HTML wireframe.

Use a modern Geist-like neo-grotesk throughout, Geist Mono-like type only in code and terminal UI, a small geometric PayPerQ wordmark with its orange flame. No serif accents.
