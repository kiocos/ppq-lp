# Review rounds

**r1**
- Bar hot end to end: a negative `dt` on slow frames blew up the diffusion. Fixed by clamping `dt` and the intro.
- A full-width bar read as a section rule. It became a finite workpiece with a forged point under the copy.
- Flat band and big dark blotches. Added a top face, corner and underside shading, and smaller oxide flecks.
- Sparks read like comic impact lines. Made them thinner, with staggered starts, drag and gravity.
- On a phone the bar was below the fold. On narrow screens the bar now sits between the copy and the card.
- The anatomy legend covered pin 1. It became a 2×2 strip under the bar.

**r2**
- A cursor hot spot became white with a light column above it. Cursor heat is now capped at 0.82, and the glow is blurred wider as it rises.
- Switching to API shrank the card and moved the layout. Both views now have the same height.
- A long thin point read as a blade. It is shorter and blunter (170 px, tip at 20%).
- Hot tokens stayed orange for too long. Cooling is now 1.05 s with a desaturating step.
