Four close matches, followed by two more dramatic alternatives. All six were inspected live in their dark presentation.

1 · Closest to Luma

FLORA ↗

My strongest aesthetic match

Black canvas, expressive serif headline, italic emphasis, and a loose composition of richly colored images. The surrounding interface stays quiet.

How this fits PPQ

Use a small set of striking PPQ outputs around a clear headline. Let the examples provide the color and mood.

The node-based workflow belongs to FLORA; PPQ’s demonstration should use its own chat and model-selection interface.

2 · Closest to Jitter

Framer ↗

Best structural reference

Large white sans-serif type, ample black space, compact navigation, and a broad product demonstration beneath the opening copy.

How this fits PPQ

Use this spacing and hierarchy for the PPQ opening: one strong message, a clear action, and a substantial real-product preview.

Its current page is more businesslike than Jitter. Carry over Jitter’s playfulness through the showcased outputs.

3 · Jitter’s simplicity

Spline ↗

Best prompt-first reference

A minimal black shell, restrained gray controls, a prominent prompt field, example chips, and large scenes of the working editor farther down.

How this fits PPQ

A particularly direct fit for PPQ’s existing prompt entry. Follow it with a legible demonstration of switching or comparing models.

Keep text contrast strong; some animated headline states are intentionally very dim.

4 · Luma’s editorial character

Unicorn Studio ↗

Best typography and texture reference

Charcoal surfaces, an oversized expressive headline, muted supporting copy, and a colorful visual canvas. Decorative drawing details add a crafted character.

How this fits PPQ

Use its balance of expressive imagery and neutral UI. It is a useful reference if PPQ should feel like a creative instrument.

Its decorative frame is a brand-specific device. PPQ needs its own visual motif.

5 · More theatrical alternative

Huly ↗

Useful for atmosphere

Oversized white headings, a dark atmospheric backdrop, a luminous vertical composition and a product window entering at the bottom.

How this fits PPQ

Study the transition from a big visual idea into the working interface. Its warm CTA accent is relevant to PPQ’s orange.

The bloom and glow dominate more than they do on Jitter or Luma. This is a stronger effects direction.

6 · Motion and result-first storytelling

Rive ↗

Useful for demonstrations

A black navigation shell and large motion examples with readable labels. The current version uses angular display type and a more technical layout.

How this fits PPQ

Use a short, labeled example of what the product produces. Connect moving visuals to real capabilities.

Its current typography and density are farther from the restrained Jitter/Luma direction.
