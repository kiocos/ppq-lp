# Provider and model icons

These local assets identify providers and models in the pricing catalog, chat selector, comparison controls and conversation search. Existing shared assets are reused where suitable. Logos and publisher avatars remain the property of their owners.

Lobe Icons SVGs are from commit `8a1b2821bb768c484fe8da20c5e1838b6bf551a9` (MIT; see `LICENSE.lobe-icons`). Simple Icons assets are from v15.16.0 (CC0; see `LICENSE.simple-icons`). Other assets come from the publisher website or its Hugging Face profile, except the transparent China Telecom mark (CompaniesLogo; verified against the official favicon and XingChen’s publisher profile).

Reflection’s favicon background was removed and the unchanged mark centered in its SVG viewBox. CSS inverts monochrome marks or brightens dark colors in dark mode; publisher artwork is otherwise unchanged.
