Your chat traffic stays unreadable to us.

PPQ routes chat requests through a secure enclave: an isolated hardware environment that keeps your prompts and replies out of our ordinary servers and logs.

We receive the metadata needed to route and bill for your requests. The contents of your chat traffic remain protected from us.

Built to be checked

Published enclave code, reproducible builds, and independent verification tools let you examine the protection behind the promise.

03↘

Know what your model provider can see.

Protection from PPQ is one part of the picture. Your model’s privacy label explains what happens when the request reaches its provider.

Anon

The provider can read your prompt and may retain it under its policies.

Zero Data Retention

The provider processes your request under an agreement not to store prompts or replies.

End-to-end encrypted models

The model itself runs inside a protected enclave, shielding the conversation’s contents from its hosting provider.

Compare privacy protections

API protections depend on configuration and supported features. Zero Data Retention must be enabled for API requests.
