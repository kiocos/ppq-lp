Enclave

Your chat traffic stays unreadable to us.

PPQ routes chat requests through a secure enclave: an isolated hardware environment that keeps your prompts and replies out of our ordinary servers and logs.

We receive the metadata needed to route and bill for your requests. The contents of your chat traffic remain protected from us.

Built to be checked

Published enclave code, reproducible builds, and independent verification tools let you examine the protection behind the promise.
