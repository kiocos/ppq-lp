Limits

The fine print, in large print.

A privacy claim that hides its edges isn’t worth much. Here are ours.

  • Media isn’t in the enclave yet.

    Image, video, music, speech, and research tools still run on our backend. We don’t log them, but we can’t prove it yet.

  • Cards leave a trail.

    Card details are tied to your credit id at PayPerQ, never at the provider. Pay with crypto if that matters to you.

  • Your IP reaches our edge.

    Like any web request. Use a VPN if you want it hidden too.

  • OpenAI asks for a customer tag.*

    The enclave sends a keyed hash of your credit id. OpenAI can group your requests, not identify you.

  • This isn’t zero trust.

    You still trust Amazon’s Nitro hardware and the reviewers who checked it. You no longer have to trust us.
