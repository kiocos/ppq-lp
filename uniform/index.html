PayPerQ landing studies: Uniform

Nine directions for “Chat and build privately”. Each one keeps a familiar structure (hero, privacy, models, API, pricing, close) and changes what surprises inside it. Directions 01–07 went through three rounds (round 3 is current); 08 Direct has two versions (v1 plain, v2 with motion). 09 Prometheus has eleven rounds (round 11, with the composer in the hero, is current) and a re-take on paper (The Theft). 06 Darkroom also has a daylight re-take, and 07 Garage a light-only re-take (Threshold). The chat footage is the real app on localhost; the conversations are written examples.

On any page, press T or use the Aa button to switch between its three type pairings. The choice is kept in the URL (?type=2).

06 Darkroom warm black · safelight amber · Geist

PayPerQ’s orange as the safelight, the one light you can work under. Noise is the medium: the real chat develops out of grain, the typed prompt is sealed back into noise, PayPerQ relays only noise (model, tokens and time stay legible), and the answer develops on screen. 07 Garage warm graphite · amber light · Barlow Condensed

You’re building something nobody has seen yet: the door is down and a strip of amber light shows under it. The light flickers and someone crosses the beam. “Start chatting” rolls the door up on the real chat. The light edge recurs under cards, the code window, the active nav item and the section headings, and the models sit on a pegboard as tool shadows. The Threshold re-take drops the drawn door and keeps only the light; it showed the door is what carries the story.

08 Direct black · PPQ orange · Archivo Semi-Expanded

No metaphor and short copy, with more life than v1. The real chat sits on a warm, grainy orange panel. The buttons are lit and tactile, with a sheen on hover. Each idea has one motion that explains it: your message turns into noise on the “PayPerQ sees” card, a strip of real model names moves, the code swaps its base URL and key, and “$0.10 ≈ 43 messages” counts up.

09 Prometheus warm black · PPQ brand orange · Geist Regular

A 17th-century-style oil painting of Prometheus carrying the fire down the mountain sits behind the hero at 42% opacity: a background reference, not the subject. The copy is the approved PPQ lines (“Chat and build privately.”). Round 3 follows your notes: calm type at the reference’s sizes (52 px regular headline, 18 px body), flat 6 px buttons, PPQ’s brand orange from the app’s theme (#bf3c1b) instead of the lighter orange, and the app’s own privacy panel in place of the painted detail. Only the hero and the closing valley use paintings. Rounds 1–2 had “Take the fire.” and an ember flicker; The Theft re-take used “Steal the fire.” and paper sections.
