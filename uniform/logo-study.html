Recommendation

Retire Orbitron. Its letters belong to sci-fi and gaming, which undercuts the stable, precise tone in the brief. The width is worth keeping, though: it’s what people recognise.

  1. Archivo Expanded Bold, if continuity matters. The same wide stance, without genre cues. It pairs with Geist for everything else, as on page 02.
  2. Geist SemiBold, for maximum restraint. The wordmark disappears into the system, as Vercel’s does, and the flame does the work. It suits pages 01 and 03.
  3. Avoid Michroma. It improves the drawing but stays in the same genre, so it doesn’t solve the problem.
