Privacy

Even we can’t read your chats.

Follow your message from your device to the model, and see where it stays private.

  1. 01 Your device

    Encrypted before it leaves your device.

    Your browser encrypts your message. Only the secure enclave can decrypt it.

  2. 02 PayPerQ servers

    We pass it on, still encrypted.

    Our servers route your request without reading it. We can see your IP address, model and token counts.

  3. 03 Inside the enclave

    Decrypted only inside the enclave.

    Your message is opened inside an isolated AWS Nitro Enclave. No one at PayPerQ can log in or read it.

  4. 04 The AI provider

    Your message goes to the model. Your IP stays behind.

    The enclave sends your request to the model you choose. What the provider keeps depends on that model’s privacy policy.

  5. 05 Back in your browser

    Your browser verifies the code.

    Your browser checks AWS-signed proof against our open-source code, so you can verify where your message is decrypted.

Technical details for the curious
Encryption
TLS ends inside the enclave, so our servers only relay ciphertext. They do see your IP address, the model and token counts.
Attestation
AWS signs the enclave’s measurement (PCR0). It must match the reproducible build of our open-source code, v0.75.0 (Nov 13, 2026).
