# Forge retake prompts

Generated independently with the built-in image generation tool, without reference images. Each output is a 1672 × 941 PNG, approximately 16:9. The tool chose the output resolution after being asked for the largest available size.

## forge2-midcentury.png

```text
Create a landscape 16:9 hero background at the largest available resolution. A true 1950s–60s mid-century modern flat illustration, like vintage industrial posters and children's-book art of that era: bold flat geometric shapes, absolutely no gradients or realistic rendering. A calm, dignified, fully clothed craftsperson in work clothes and leather apron works glowing metal at a small contained forge, facing left. Build the almost faceless figure from simple rectangles, triangles and circles. Place the figure, forge and metal entirely in the right 40%; leave the left 55–60% near-black and empty for a white headline. Limited palette of near-black, charcoal, warm grey, and one flat red-orange close to #bf3c1b with a hotter core. Screenprint grain and slight misregistration. Unmistakably flat printed artwork at thumbnail size, not a photo or realistic digital illustration. No blazes, smoke clouds, spark showers, text, letters, numbers, signatures, watermarks, frames or borders.
```

Differences from the brief: the apron and gloves use saturated orange beyond the hot metal and forge; the anvil reaches slightly into the left half. The flat, faceless figure and print texture are distinct at thumbnail size.

## forge2-engraving.png

```text

Invert the plate so its lines are pale on a black ground. Only the hot metal and the mouth of a small contained forge are hand-coloured in orange watercolour, close to #bf3c1b with a hotter core. A small fully clothed craftsperson in work clothes and leather apron calmly works the metal, facing left; tools on the wall arranged like a technical plate. Put all figure, forge, tools and metal in the right 40%, leaving the left 55–60% near-black and empty for a white headline. The image must read unmistakably as an etched print, not a photo or realistic digital illustration. No blazes, smoke clouds, spark showers, caption, text, letters, numbers, signatures, watermarks, frames or borders.
```

Differences from the brief: the figure is relatively large rather than small, and the forge and bucket reach just left of the 55–60% boundary. The plate uses detailed engraved linework, with orange restricted to the forge and metal.

All three outputs were inspected. None appeared to be a realistic digital illustration or photograph, so no regeneration was needed.
