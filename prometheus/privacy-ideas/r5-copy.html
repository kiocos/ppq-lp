Privacy

Your chats run inside AWS Nitro Enclaves.
Even we can’t read them.

Follow one message from your keyboard to the model, and see who can read it along the way.

01

TLS runs from your device straight into the enclave.

On most AI services, TLS ends at the company’s servers, and your prompt is readable there in plain text.

02

Our servers only relay ciphertext.

We see the metadata: your IP address, the model, the token count. Never a word you wrote.

03

It’s decrypted only inside the AWS Nitro Enclave.

Nitro hardware cuts it off from the server it runs on: no SSH, no disk, no root access. Our engineers can’t look inside.

04

The model provider gets your prompt, not your IP.

The enclave forwards it to the provider you picked, like Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. What they keep depends on each model’s privacy label.

05

Don’t trust us. Check the hash.

AWS signs a hash of the exact code running in the enclave, called PCR0. Your browser compares it with our open-source build on GitHub, which anyone can reproduce.

06

Or check it from your terminal.

Our open-source verifier reads the AWS attestation itself, and refuses to send anything if the hash doesn’t match.
